# Makefield Works

Makefield Works is a one-person software workshop in Upper Makefield Township, Pennsylvania, United States. Its first tool helped sailors plan ocean crossings. Today the workshop also builds software for federal retirement, equipment records, and shared garden notebooks.

The workshop favors local-first software. Wherever practical, you can work through a question and keep the results on your device without creating an account.

## Software from the workshop

| Product | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [FERSCalc](https://ferscalc.com/) | Compare federal retirement dates and see how your pension, TSP withdrawals, and Social Security fit together. |
| [Visual Passage Planner](https://www.digwave.com/) | Lay out an ocean passage and compare the typical winds, currents, and waves along your route across different departure months. |
| [NautiCalc](https://www.nauticalc.com/) | Estimate arrival time, calculate fuel range, or work out how much anchor line to let out. The calculators also work offline. |
| [Statekeep](https://statekeep.app/) | Record how a lab or installation is configured. Compare two saved snapshots to see which components moved and which settings changed. |
| [The Grunting Gardener](https://thegruntinggardener.com/) | Keep a garden notebook with friends. Share a harvest photo, ask a planting question, and find the advice again next season. |
