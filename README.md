# Makefield Works

> **Careful tools for planning, one at a time.**

Makefield Works is a small, independent software workshop in Washington Crossing, Pennsylvania. We make focused tools for people working through consequential decisions—whether they are choosing a federal retirement date, planning an offshore passage, keeping a complex project intelligible, or mapping next season's garden.

Our products are independent by design. Each has its own name, audience, and domain; what they share is a preference for useful clarity over unnecessary ceremony.

## What we believe

Planning software should help people see a decision more clearly, then get out of the way.

- **Local-first by default.** Your salary figures, route ideas, project notes, and garden plans belong to you. We avoid accounts and hosted services when they do not make the tool genuinely better.
- **Built from use.** We start with real problems: a retirement decision to make, an ocean to cross, or complicated work to keep organized—not a list of keywords or features.
- **Quiet by design.** A clearer picture matters more than a louder interface. If a feature does not help someone make a better decision, it does not earn its place.
- **Honest scope.** We build small, durable tools around a specific job, rather than trying to turn every problem into a platform.
- **Fair ownership.** Where possible, products are free or available for a one-time price—no subscription treadmill for the sake of it.

## Open source

### [Manifest](https://github.com/MakefieldWorks/Manifest)

Manifest is our open-source project for teams and individuals who want project structure and history without surrendering their data to a hosted service. Projects remain readable on disk; Git supports named snapshots; and meaningful changes can be compared as moves, renames, reordered nodes, and property changes—not just raw text churn.

It is built with Electron, TypeScript, Svelte, Tailwind, SQLite FTS5, and Git, and is released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/MakefieldWorks/Manifest/blob/main/LICENSE).

## A little history

The first product in this lineage was Visual Passage Planner, created under Digital Wave more than thirty years ago. After a career shift from full-time work, the workshop widened to include retirement planning, project-structure tools, and a future garden planner.

Makefield Works is the quiet parent brand for that broader family: it preserves Visual Passage Planner's history, gives newer projects a shared home, and lets each product remain itself.
